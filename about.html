# writing about code, tools, and the things in between

home about rss youtube github

Hey, I'm glad you found this. So here's the thing about me — I'm a Software Engineer from India. I've always been a little obsessed with the idea that technology used to be simple, and somewhere along the way we let it get complicated on purpose. Not because it had to be. Because it was profitable for it to be. I'm big into the Unix philosophy — the old idea that a tool should do one thing, do it well, and get out of your way. No bloat, no dark patterns, no fifteen background processes phoning home to sell your attention to the highest bidder. I think that idea is quietly radical, and I think most people would love their tech a lot more if it actually worked that way. That's basically what this blog is about. I explain Linux and "hard" tech stuff in plain English — no gatekeeping, no assuming you already know what a kernel is. I'm into digital privacy, not in a tinfoil-hat way, just in a "I'd like to own my own stuff" way. And I care about digital minimalism — not as some aesthetic with a beige desktop, but as an actual practice of using fewer tools, chosen on purpose, instead of forty apps chosen for you. If any of that sounds like your kind of rabbit hole, welcome. Grab a coffee, poke around, and let's make our technology boring, simple, and ours again.
